# Existing Tabs and Screenshot Notes

These are descriptions of current screens, not captured screenshots.

## Financial Reports Notebook

- General Ledger: date/account filters above a ledger table and report actions.
- Balance Sheet: account balances table and report actions.
- Lebanese VAT Report: currency/type/invoice count/before-VAT/VAT/total table with summary.
- Cash Flow: cash/bank movement grouped by currency and cashflow category.
- Cash Flow Outlook: projected monthly cash movement and a long-term annual projection section.
- Cash Budget: base year, target year, inflow/outflow growth, monthly opening/inflow/outflow/net/closing table, and exports.
- Comparative P&L: comparative income statement report.
- Comparative Trial Balance: comparative account balances.
- Ageing Report: customer/supplier ageing controls and outstanding-balance table.
- Budget: editable annual/monthly account lines, project-from-actual, five-year projection controls, and budget-vs-actual output.
- Projection & Budget (3D): base year/currency/scenario assumptions, editable growth settings, charts, and export actions.
- Business Reports: report selector and filters above a generated report viewer.
- Management Pack: period/currency selection with management report and export actions.
